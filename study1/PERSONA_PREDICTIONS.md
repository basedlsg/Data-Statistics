**Study**: Next-Generation Agent Performance Study
**Date**: 2025-11-22
**Design**: 5 (Risk Tolerance) × 5 (Self-Monitoring) × 2 (Domain) factorial

## Executive Summary

Based on pilot study findings (Seshat positive, Ma'at negative), we predict:

### Primary Hypotheses

**H1: Self-Monitoring Main Effect**
- High self-monitoring (levels 4-5) will outperform low self-monitoring (levels 1-2)
- **Expected effect size**: d = 0.6-0.8

**H2: Domain Main Effect**
- Technical domain will outperform creative domain
- **Expected effect size**: d = 0.4-0.6

**H3: Self-Monitoring × Domain Interaction**
- Self-monitoring effect will be strongest in technical domain
- Creative domain may show inverted U-curve (moderate monitoring optimal)
- **Expected interaction**: F > 4.0, p < 0.05

**H4: Risk Tolerance Curvilinear Effect**
- Moderate risk (levels 3-4) will outperform extremes (1, 5)
- Ultra-conservative (1) and ultra-risk (5) will show poorest performance
- **Expected quadratic term**: β² < 0, p < 0.05

## Statistical Predictions

### Regression Model

We predict the following regression coefficients:

```
Effect ~ β₀ + β₁(Risk) + β₂(Risk²) + β₃(Monitor) + β₄(Domain) +
         β₅(Risk×Monitor) + β₆(Risk×Domain) + β₇(Monitor×Domain) + ε
```

**Expected coefficients:**
- β₀ (Intercept): -0.30
- β₁ (Risk): +0.25**
- β₂ (Risk²): -0.05* (inverted U)
- β₃ (Monitor): +0.18***
- β₄ (Domain_technical): +0.35***
- β₅ (Risk×Monitor): +0.03
- β₆ (Risk×Domain): -0.02
- β₇ (Monitor×Domain): +0.08**

**R² expected**: 0.65-0.75

### ANOVA Predictions

**Main effects:**
- Risk Tolerance: F(4,45) ≈ 8.5, p < 0.001, η² ≈ 0.25
- Self-Monitoring: F(4,45) ≈ 15.2, p < 0.001, η² ≈ 0.40
- Domain: F(1,48) ≈ 22.1, p < 0.001, η² ≈ 0.30

**Interactions:**
- Monitor×Domain: F(4,45) ≈ 4.8, p < 0.01, η² ≈ 0.15
- Risk×Domain: F(4,45) ≈ 2.3, p < 0.10, η² ≈ 0.08
- Risk×Monitor: F(16,45) ≈ 1.5, p > 0.10, η² ≈ 0.05

## Robustness Checks

### Alternative Outcome Measures

If primary effect measure differs from pilot, we expect:

**Task completion rate:**
- Higher for low-monitoring personas (faster but lower quality)
- Risk-tolerant personas complete more tasks

**Error rate:**
- Inversely correlated with self-monitoring
- Technical domain shows lower error rate

**Time to completion:**
- Faster for risk-tolerant, low-monitoring personas
- Slower for ultra-conservative, high-monitoring personas

**Quality score:**
- Highest for moderate-risk, high-monitoring, technical personas
- Lowest for ultra-risk, low-monitoring, creative personas

### Sensitivity Analysis

**If self-monitoring effect is weaker than expected:**
- Risk tolerance and domain will show stronger main effects
- Optimal persona shifts toward moderate-risk technical regardless of monitoring

**If domain effect is weaker than expected:**
- Self-monitoring becomes dominant predictor
- Creative personas with high monitoring may approach technical performance

**If risk tolerance shows linear rather than quadratic effect:**
- Higher risk tolerance consistently better (contradicts prediction)
- Would suggest safety features in environment buffer recklessness

---

## Falsification Criteria

These results would **falsify our theory**:

1. **Low monitoring outperforms high monitoring** (contradicts pilot)
2. **Creative domain outperforms technical domain** (contradicts pilot)
3. **No interaction between monitoring and domain** (contradicts theory)
4. **Ultra-risk personas perform best overall** (suggests no penalty for recklessness)
5. **Monitoring shows no effect in technical domain** (contradicts Seshat success)

## Experimental Protocol Notes

### Sample Size
- N=50 personas × 10 tasks each = 500 total observations
- Power analysis: 80% power to detect d=0.5 at α=0.05

### Task Selection
- Use same tasks as pilot study for comparability
- Mix of technical and creative tasks
- Standardized difficulty and scoring

### Randomization
- Random assignment of tasks to personas
- Counterbalance task order
- Block by domain to ensure balanced comparison

### Blinding
- Scorers blind to persona condition
- Automated scoring where possible
- Inter-rater reliability κ > 0.75
