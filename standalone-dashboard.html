Regional Investment Preferences

Bay Area Focus on vision, charisma, AI/infrastructure. High hype elasticity, tolerant of pre-revenue.

NYC Emphasis on revenue, enterprise/fintech. Moderate hype elasticity, fundamentals-driven.

Boston Bio/deeptech focus, science signals, repeat founders. Low hype elasticity.

LA Consumer/media emphasis, brand and story tilt. High hype elasticity.

Simulation Parameters

200 founder agents with multi-dimensional feature vectors (revenue, growth, charisma, vision, domain)

Scoring model: score = w_region • features + β_region * Hype(t) + ε

Hype states: Risk-off, Normal, Hype (3-state Markov chain)

Deterministic random seeds ensure reproducibility
