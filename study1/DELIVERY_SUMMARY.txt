ERROR HANDLING MATRIX
----------------------
Error Type          | Detection              | Response           | Retry
--------------------|------------------------|--------------------|---------
SSL Error           | httpx.ConnectError     | Backoff 2^attempt  | Yes (2x)
Timeout             | TimeoutException       | Backoff 2^attempt  | Yes (2x)
Rate Limit          | '429' or 'rate_limit'  | Backoff 5s         | Yes (2x)
Connection Failure  | Network errors         | Backoff 2^attempt  | Yes (2x)
Invalid Response    | Generic Exception      | Log & fallback     | Yes (2x)
Safety Block        | 'safety' in error      | Log only           | No

RETRY STRATEGY
--------------
Attempt 1: Immediate
Attempt 2: Wait 1 second (2^0)
Attempt 3: Wait 2 seconds (2^1) [if max_retries > 2]
Rate Limit Special: Wait 5 seconds * attempt_number
